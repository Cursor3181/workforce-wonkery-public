# Public Workforce Wonkery agent instructions

Reader-facing source is developed here, while the protected Workforce Wonkery repository remains authoritative for editorial research, source evidence, privacy, publication approvals, Publisher transaction receipts and newsletter controls.

**Read docs/decoded-reader-contract.md before working on Policy, Data or Practice content, previews, components or page templates.** The Decoded brand promise requires understandable evidence, a short useful insight, a readable story and a practical next action. **Write as a smart friend and mentor: lead with a genuine question, interpret the surprising part of the evidence in a memorable summary, acknowledge limits naturally, and end with a useful move. Avoid the cold voice of a technical manual or checklist.** For Practice resources use the three-depth reading experience and make Desk Aid inputs, outputs and decision boundaries immediately clear.

Preserve the approved site-wide visual language, existing source links, accessibility and mobile rendering. Avoid decorative redesign, invented quotes, generic AI marketing language, imitation of living writers and claims of effectiveness beyond the supplied sources.

Develop in a public feature branch, pass public CI and review. Public main merge does not publish WordPress; use the approved private import and Publisher. No user data, secrets, private research drafts or credentials may be moved into this public repository.
