About the {{AREA_SHORT_NAME}} tract explorer

Source: California Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment / CalEPA, CalEnviroScreen 5.0, released July 1, 2026. {{SOURCE_SCOPE_TEXT}}

Workforce geography: the browser uses 2020 U.S. Census incorporated-place boundaries for {{MEMBER_CITY_TEXT}}. A tract is assigned to the workforce area when the center of its displayed tract geometry falls inside a member-city boundary. Border tracts can cross city lines, so the tract number remains the precise data geography.

Location context: selected tracts are enriched with 2020 Census incorporated-place, census-designated-place, county-subdivision, and ZIP Code Tabulation Area context. Local official planning-area layers may override the generic label where a governed area-specific source is available. These labels describe the tract center and are not formal neighborhood or tract boundaries.

Phase 1 scope: six workforce-relevant condition measures and seven descriptive community-composition measures. No WIOA participant, service, case-management, or outcome records are used. No composite equity score or neighborhood ranking is produced.

Next enrichment gate: add validated 2020–2024 American Community Survey tract precision measures only after an {{AREA_SHORT_NAME}} static package passes coverage, margin-of-error, geography, and public-packaging QA.
