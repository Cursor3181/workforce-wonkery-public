The job in plain English

You maintain the bridge between training providers and the workforce system.

The role is not just data entry. Good ETPL coordination blends provider support, policy review, CalJOBS administration, performance reporting, and compliance judgment.

Guide

Help providers understand the process and what they need to submit.

Reviewer

Check provider and program information against state, federal, and local requirements.

Data steward

Keep CalJOBS information, status, costs, credentials, and performance records accurate.

Compliance steward

Track continued eligibility, reporting issues, corrective action, and removal risks.

Connector

Coordinate among providers, the local board, EDD, program staff, and other partners.

Important boundary: ETPL eligibility makes a program potentially available for WIOA-funded training. It does not by itself approve an individual participant for training or guarantee WIOA funding.

What do I do if…?

Troubleshooting without guessing

Use these as triage prompts. High-consequence eligibility or removal decisions should always be checked against the current official source and local policy.

A provider wants to get on the ETPL. Where do I start?

Start with the provider, not the individual course. Confirm which provider pathway applies, what approvals and information are required, and whether the provider record is ready before reviewing programs.

A program is already in CalJOBS, but something is wrong.

Identify whether the problem is provider data, program data, performance data, system permissions, status, or a state-side issue. Correct only what your role is authorized to change and document the unresolved item before escalation.

A career planner says a participant wants a program that is not on the ETPL.

Do not treat this as an automatic yes or no. Determine whether the program is actually required to be on the ETPL, whether reciprocity or another exception applies, and whether local training policy allows the proposed path.

The provider is missing performance information.

Determine which reporting requirement and program year apply. Missing required data can affect continued eligibility, so use the current EDD notice and reporting instructions rather than relying on last year's dates.

The provider changed tuition, credential, ownership, or approval status.

Treat material changes as a review trigger. Determine what must be updated in CalJOBS, whether eligibility is affected, and whether EDD or another approving body must act before the record is treated as current.

I cannot tell whether this is a local decision or an EDD decision.

Use the responsibility chain above, then check the controlling directive. If the directive does not clearly delegate the decision to the local area, escalate rather than inventing a local rule.

Source stack

Know which rule controls.

Workforce Wonkery should translate the rule, not replace it. Use the newest applicable authority when sources conflict.

  • California ETPL directive: WSD25-02 and its attachments.
  • Federal ETPL framework: TEGL 08-19 and Change 1.
  • Current EDD notices: use relevant WSINs for reporting windows, implementation details, and time-sensitive instructions.
  • Reciprocity: WSD22-08 brief.
  • Participant training decision: Approve a Training Request.
  • Policy updates: ETPL Policy Briefs.
Rule of thumb: If the decision can affect a provider's eligibility, a participant's access to funding, a formal removal, repayment, or an appeal right, verify the current official authority before acting.
