How to use this library

Get the most out of the briefs

Search an identifier or topic. Select a title or Read brief for analysis; use Official source for the underlying authority.

How to read a brief

Check status and date. Final, draft, pending, superseded, rescinded, and historical material should not be treated the same way.

Use the official source. The brief is an orientation layer. The underlying authority remains controlling.

Separate rules from interpretation. Examples, scenarios, and good-practice ideas are not additional government requirements.

Verify high-consequence decisions locally. Eligibility, fiscal, procurement, contracting, monitoring, civil-rights, personnel, and deadline questions may depend on local rules or professional advice.
