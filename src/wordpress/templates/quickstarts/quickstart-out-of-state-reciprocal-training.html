Handle an Out-of-State or Reciprocal Training Program

Use this when a provider or participant wants to use training located outside California, or an online provider has no California location.

Before you act: This playbook translates current California and federal ETPL rules into an operating sequence. Official sources, the current CalJOBS workflow, and your adopted local policy control. Source check: October 7, 2026.
Use this when

The proposed training is out of state, online-only with no California location, or relies on a reciprocal ETPL arrangement.

You need to decide

Which route applies: California State online-provider route, California program-location review, or an approved interstate reciprocal agreement.

Verify against

WSD25-02, WSD22-08, the current reciprocal-agreement status, provider/program eligibility in the other state, local ITA policy, and the participant training decision.

Have these ready

Provider headquarters · all training locations · delivery mode · other-state ETPL listing · reciprocal agreement evidence · program identity · participant's proposed training plan · local ITA policy.

60-second pathMap provider/program geography → identify online vs physical route → verify reciprocal agreement if needed → verify exact provider/program → apply participant/local ITA rules → document decision.

1. Map the geography first

“Out of state” can describe several different situations. The route changes depending on where the provider is headquartered, where the program is physically delivered, and whether there is any California location.

2. Online provider with no California location

WSD25-02 directs providers with no California location that offer online training to California students to the State ETPL Coordinator. Local staff should document and route the case rather than invent a local provider eligibility review.

3. Physical training located only outside California

Do not assume the provider can be placed on the California ETPL just because another state lists it. WSD22-08 governs use of an approved reciprocal agreement. Without an applicable approved agreement, a standard California ITA cannot simply rely on another state's list.

4. Verify the reciprocal agreement and the exact program

  1. Confirm a current reciprocal agreement covers the other state.
  2. Verify the exact provider and program are eligible under that state's list and the agreement.
  3. Confirm any licensing/approval conditions that apply to the training.
  4. Retain evidence of the eligibility check.
Not blanket permission: Reciprocity expands recognition across state lines. It does not automatically approve the participant, guarantee WIOA funding, waive local ITA policy, or make every program on another state's website payable.

5. Apply the participant training decision separately

After the program route is valid, the career planner still needs to determine whether the training is appropriate for the participant, fits the employment plan, meets local ITA rules, has available funding, and reflects informed customer choice.

6. Document travel and access realities

If training is in person outside California, document commuting, relocation, schedule, supportive-service implications, and the participant's ability to complete the program. These are participant/service-design questions, not substitutes for ETPL eligibility.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming another state's ETPL listing automatically works in California.
  • Sending every online provider through a Local Board review even when WSD25-02 assigns the route to the State.
  • Confusing provider headquarters with program location.
  • Ignoring participant/local ITA requirements after reciprocity is confirmed.

Stop and escalate when

  • No current reciprocal agreement can be confirmed.
  • The other state's program status is unclear or expired.
  • The provider's delivery model does not match the record.
  • The program has no California location and State routing is required.
  • Staff are being asked to commit WIOA funds before eligibility and participant approval are verified.
