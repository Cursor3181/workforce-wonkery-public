Review a Registered Apprenticeship for ETPL

Use this when a Registered Apprenticeship Program sponsor asks about California ETPL listing or a Local Board encounters a registered apprenticeship in an ETPL case.

Before you act: This playbook translates current California and federal ETPL rules into an operating sequence. Official sources, the current CalJOBS workflow, and your adopted local policy control. Source check: October 7, 2026.
Use this when

The program is a bona fide Registered Apprenticeship Program registered under the National Apprenticeship Act or a recognized State apprenticeship agency.

You need to decide

Whether it is truly a registered apprenticeship, whether the sponsor consents to ETPL inclusion, and what information must be routed to EDD without adding local eligibility gates.

Verify against

WSD25-02 RAP provisions, TEGL 08-19 and Change 1, current apprenticeship registration evidence, and current EDD ETPL procedures.

Have these ready

Program/sponsor legal name · registration evidence/identifier · occupations · program locations · sponsor contact · consent/listing information · existing CalJOBS/ETPL status if any.

60-second pathVerify RAP status → confirm sponsor/listing information → route through the State RAP pathway → do not apply ordinary State/local eligibility tests → verify listing → keep recruiting/capacity questions separate.

1. Verify that it is actually a Registered Apprenticeship

Pre-apprenticeship, employer training, internships, and unregistered earn-and-learn programs are not the same thing. Verify registration through the appropriate federal or State apprenticeship authority.

2. Understand the special ETPL rule

Federal WIOA guidance makes Registered Apprenticeship Programs automatically eligible for inclusion on the ETP list. California uses a special State pathway for RAPs rather than the ordinary Local Board provider/program eligibility review.

Do not add local eligibility gates. A Local Board should not require the RAP to pass the ordinary provider/program performance criteria or invent additional data requirements as a condition of the California ETPL listing.

3. Keep listing, recruiting, and capacity separate

QuestionWhat it means
Is it a registered apprenticeship?Registration/status question.
Is it on the ETPL?Listing/consent/State process question.
Is it accepting applicants?Recruiting question.
Can a participant actually obtain a sponsored/dispatch slot?Capacity/access question.

Do not promise a participant an apprenticeship slot merely because the RAP appears on the ETPL.

4. Route to EDD when the State owns the step

Use the current State ETPL Coordinator process for RAP inclusion, corrections, and listing questions. Document the referral and State response rather than recreating an ordinary local review.

5. Performance reporting is different for RAPs

WSD25-02 treats RAPs differently from conventional ETP programs, including exemptions from required ETP performance reporting. Do not impose an additional mandatory local performance submission as a condition of the California ETPL status unless you have clear lawful authority for a separate local purpose.

6. Verify the live listing

After the State action, confirm the RAP appears correctly in CalJOBS/ETPL and that sponsor/program information is current.

Common mistakes

  • Treating pre-apprenticeship as Registered Apprenticeship.
  • Running a RAP through the normal Local Board eligibility checklist.
  • Demanding the same annual ETP performance submission as a conventional provider.
  • Assuming “listed” means “currently recruiting” or “has capacity.”
  • Using ETPL listing as proof that a specific participant is eligible for apprenticeship-related WIOA funding.

Stop and escalate when

  • Registration status cannot be verified.
  • The sponsor disputes whether it consented to ETPL inclusion.
  • The RAP record is wrong or missing in the State system.
  • A local policy appears to impose additional RAP eligibility requirements.
