Ask the ETPL Desk

A source-grounded beta for common ETPL questions. It searches curated answers and routes you to the governing playbook instead of inventing a policy answer.

Why this is intentionally constrained: this is not an open-ended AI legal/compliance bot. It returns only curated answers tied to known ETPL workflows and sources. If it does not recognize the question, it tells you to verify or escalate.

When the Desk should refuse to guess

  • The current directive or notice is not in the governed source set.
  • The question turns on a local policy that has not been verified.
  • The live CalJOBS screen contradicts the walkthrough.
  • The facts could trigger removal, repayment, appeal, fraud/incident reporting, or participant harm.
  • The user asks for a determination without the fact that controls the rule.
