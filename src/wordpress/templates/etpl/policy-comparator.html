State vs. Local Policy Comparator

See which layer of authority controls a decision before a local practice accidentally becomes a State rule.

Core rule: California ETPL eligibility, any separate local ETPL decision, and participant ITA/training policy are different layers. The comparator labels those layers instead of blending them.

Local policy review worksheet

1 · Identify

What local document controls?

Policy number/title, adoption date, revision date, board approval evidence, and current effective version.

2 · Classify

Which layer does each rule belong to?

State ETPL eligibility, separate local ETPL, participant ITA/training, monitoring, or merely internal procedure.

3 · Compare

Does local language conflict?

Flag local language that changes a State denominator, State appeal route, RAP rule, reporting requirement, or State reviewer assignment.

4 · Publish carefully

Show only verified local profiles

Workforce Wonkery should not display a local policy as authoritative until the current adopted version and approval status are verified.

Verified local-area profiles

v1 launch rule: California baseline is live first. Local-area profiles are added only after the current adopted local policy and approval evidence are verified. “No profile” means “not yet verified by Wonkery,” not “no local policy exists.”

This prevents an outdated draft, agenda attachment, or superseded policy from being presented as current local authority.

Source basis

WSD25-02 State/local decision separation; TEGL 08-19 roles/responsibilities; WSD22-08 reciprocity; 20 CFR Part 680 training/ETP framework. Local profiles require separately verified adopted policies.
