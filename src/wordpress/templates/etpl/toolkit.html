Workforce Wonkery · ETPL Coordinator Desk

ETPL Toolkit

Copyable checklists and communication starters so a new coordinator does not have to invent the administrative work.

Use these as starting points. Adapt them to the current directive, your adopted local policy, your organization's communication standards, and the facts of the case.

Coordinator checklists

Checklist 1

New provider intake checklist

Provider legal name and DBA\nHeadquarters and all California locations\nProvider type / operating-authority pathway\nPrimary ETPL contact\nCalJOBS provider record status\nRequired assurances/documents\nProposed programs and locations\nWho owns provider review?\nWho owns each program review?\nOutstanding items\nDate complete application received
Checklist 2

New program review checklist

Provider currently eligible?\nProgram name and catalog match\nPhysical/online/hybrid location\nCredential awarded\nProgram length/hours\nTotal cost and cost components\nCIP and linked occupations\nOperating approval covers program/location when applicable\nPerformance path identified\nCalJOBS submission complete\nState eligibility determination documented
Checklist 3

Continued eligibility checklist

Current provider/program ETPL history verified\nReapplication window / expiration date verified\nProvider profile current\nProgram facts current\nAnnual ETP reporting status verified\nApplicable performance measures reviewed\nMeasure-level exemptions documented\nLocal Board State-eligibility decision documented\nSeparate local standard applied only after State decision\nEDD submission and result tracked
Checklist 4

Compliance triage checklist

Exact allegation or finding\nSource evidence preserved\nKnown facts separated from assumptions\nCurrent operating authority checked\nCurrent ETPL/CalJOBS status checked\nAffected participants identified without unnecessary PII\nState vs local decision authority identified\nNotice requirements identified\nAppeal route/clock identified\nIncident-reporting trigger considered\nHuman decision-maker identified

Communication starters

New provider inquiry
Hello [Name],

Thank you for your interest in the California Eligible Training Provider List. The first step is to confirm the correct ETPL route for your organization and programs, then complete the applicable CalJOBS provider and program information.

To get started, please send or have ready: your legal/provider name, headquarters and training locations, provider type, applicable operating approval or exemption, primary contact, and the program(s) you want considered.

ETPL listing does not itself guarantee WIOA participant funding. Participant training approval is a separate process.

Thank you,
[Coordinator]
Missing information request

• [Item 1]
• [Item 2]
• [Item 3]

Please update the applicable CalJOBS record and/or provide the supporting evidence by [date, if applicable]. We will continue the review once the outstanding items are resolved.

Thank you,
[Coordinator]
Local review complete / State handoff
Hello [Name],

The Local Board has completed its California ETPL review for [Provider / Program]. The record has been [nominated/submitted] to EDD for the State review.

This local action is not the final State ETPL approval. We will track the EDD result and contact you if EDD requests a correction or when the State decision is recorded.

Thank you,
[Coordinator]
Annual reporting reminder
Hello [Name],

This is a reminder that California's annual Eligible Training Provider performance reporting requires All Student data for applicable ETPL programs. Please confirm the programs in your reporting cohort, the reporting method shown in CalJOBS, and completion of the current EDD reporting instructions.

Do not email participant SSNs or participant-level wage-match files. Use only the authorized EDD/CalJOBS process.

Please reply when the submission is complete or if you encounter an error.

Thank you,
[Coordinator]
Program change request
Hello [Name],

We received your request to change [cost / length / credential / location / delivery / other] for [Program]. Please update only the affected CalJOBS field(s) and provide the supporting evidence that shows the new value.

Before the change is treated as current, we need to reconcile the CalJOBS record with the supporting document(s) and determine whether the change affects ETPL eligibility.

Thank you,
[Coordinator]
State ETPL referral
Hello [Name],

Based on the facts provided, this issue appears to require the State ETPL Coordinator rather than a Local Board eligibility determination.

Please contact the State ETPL Coordinator at wsbetpl@edd.ca.gov and keep your local contact informed if local follow-up is still needed.

Reason for State route: [brief reason].

Thank you,
[Coordinator]

Design basis
