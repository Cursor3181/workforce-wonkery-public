What mastery means

Route correctly

You can distinguish initial, continued, reporting, RAP, reciprocity, change, compliance, and appeal workflows.

Identify the controlling fact

You know when location, prior eligibility, denominator, registration, operating authority, or decision level changes the rule.

Escalate deliberately

You can explain why a high-consequence case should stop before removal, appeal advice, fraud reporting, or participant harm.

Case basis

Cases synthesize WSD25-02, WSD22-08, WSIN26-08, TEGL 08-19/Change 1, WSD24-03, and the validated decision patterns used in the ETPL Operations Desk. They contain no real provider or participant records.
