60-Second ETPL Decision Center

Start with what happened. The router sends you to the right workflow before you read a long policy document.

Purpose: route the work, not make the final eligibility decision. When the answer depends on facts not captured here, the safe output is “verify before proceeding.”

Routing principles to memorize

Provider ≠ program

A provider can be eligible while a specific program still requires review. Keep the two decisions traceable.

State ≠ local

California ETPL eligibility is one decision. Any separate lawful local ETPL standard is a second decision.

ETPL ≠ participant approval

A listed program may be available for an ITA, but participant eligibility, training need, customer choice, local policy, and funding still matter.

RAPs are special

Registered Apprenticeship Programs use a special ETPL pathway. Do not put them through ordinary provider/program eligibility gates.

Source basis

WSD25-02 roles and responsibilities, initial/continued eligibility, RAP, and appeal provisions; WSD22-08 reciprocity; TEGL 08-19.
