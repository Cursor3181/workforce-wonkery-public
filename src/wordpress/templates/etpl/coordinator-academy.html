Your First 30 Days as an ETPL Coordinator

A practical, task-first guide for starting California ETPL duties. Follow real workflows, verify current sources, and know when to ask for help.

Orientation, not a course or credential. This guide does not certify competency, grant CalJOBS access, or authorize eligibility or enforcement decisions. Current federal and California rules, EDD directions, and your local policy control.
Use this with your manager. The suggested schedule can be adapted to your local workload and assigned authority. Do not enter identifiable participant or provider records in public practice exercises.

Days 1–3

Find the people, rules, and boundaries

  1. Identify the Local Board ETPL lead, backup, CalJOBS administrator, and State contacts. Confirm assigned review, nomination, correction, and escalation roles.
  2. Read the current California ETPL directive, federal guidance, CalJOBS instructions, and adopted local policy. Bookmark where updated instructions appear.
  3. Explain why provider eligibility, program eligibility, State determination, local policy, and participant training approval are different decisions.
Useful output: A source list, responsibility map, and questions for your manager.

Open: ETPL Desk · Policy Comparator

Week 1

Route requests before acting

  1. Use the 60-Second Decision Center to classify fictional or redacted provider, program, renewal, reporting, RAP, reciprocity, or compliance requests.
  2. For three sample requests, identify the governing source, missing evidence, owner, deadline, and next safe action.
  3. Draft a provider correction email that requests missing information without promising approval.
Useful output: Three supervisor-reviewed routes and a provider communication draft.

Open: ETPL Decision Center · Copyable Toolkit

Week 2

Work a routine review with supervision

  1. Shadow a provider review and a separate program review using authorized records or fictional examples.
  2. Use the checklist to verify operating authority, location, credential, program cost, occupation, and the applicable performance pathway against the current rules.
  3. Walk through CalJOBS Guided Mode. Do not edit live records without assigned permissions and supervisor authorization.
Useful output: A sample review checklist with documented evidence gaps and proposed next steps.

Open: CalJOBS Guided Mode · ETPL Quickstarts

Week 3

Understand reporting and the annual cycle

  1. Review the Current ETPL Desk and verify actual reporting deadlines, State notices, and pending provider changes.
  2. Practice tracing a fictional ETP reporting error and continued-eligibility issue to the right source and responsible party.
  3. Draft a 90-day owner-and-deadline tracker for your local area, then have the lead verify it.
Useful output: A locally verified task tracker that names owners, due dates, and escalation triggers.

Open: Current ETPL Desk · Provider Management

Week 4

Show sound judgment and plan a handoff

  1. Explain the next steps for routine cases using the current controlling source and the evidence in the file.
  2. Use the Case Lab to rehearse when to stop for RAPs, reciprocity, removal, appeals, suspected fraud, and ambiguous authority.
  3. With your manager, document which routine tasks are delegated, which require sign-off, and how backup coverage works.
Useful output: A manager-reviewed handoff note identifying authority, backup, and unresolved questions.

Before handing over routine work

Review these responsibilities with the authorized lead. Staff readiness is determined by actual work, source checking, supervision, and adopted local procedures, not boxes completed online.
