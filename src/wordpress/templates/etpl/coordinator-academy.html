Workforce Wonkery · ETPL Coordinator Desk

ETPL Coordinator Academy

A competency-based onboarding path for someone who was just handed the ETPL role and needs to become independently useful without pretending to know everything.

Goal: readiness, not trivia. A coordinator is ready when they can route normal cases, use CalJOBS safely, manage recurring cycles, explain the State/local boundary, and recognize when to escalate.
Academy progress: 0 of 6 levels marked complete

Start with Level 1.

Case Lab mastery: 0 of 12.

Level 1 · Understand ETPL

Explain ETPL, ETP, ITA, provider vs program, State vs local, and why ETPL listing does not approve a participant.

Use this before turning over routine ETPL work to a new coordinator.

Provider supportCan give precise correction requests and manage routine provider questions without promising approval.The issue becomes material compliance or participant harm.

Suggested manager sign-off: routine new-provider/new-program support can be delegated after Levels 1–4; independent annual-cycle administration after Level 5; high-risk cases remain escalated even after Level 6.
Academy standard

Built from the ETPL role map, Quickstarts, CalJOBS Guided Mode, WSD25-02, TEGL 08-19, annual reporting, and scenario-based judgment practice. Completion is a learning aid, not a certification issued by EDD or DOL.
