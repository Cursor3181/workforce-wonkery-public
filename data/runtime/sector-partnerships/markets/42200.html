Worker value

What workers should gain

Electrician wages exceed the local living-wage benchmarks in both labor markets. The apprenticeship provides paid entry, no tuition and progression through established local JATCs.

The move

Create one regional electrical workload view while keeping county JATC delivery and employer relationships local.

Local operating story

Regional coordination does not require regionalizing everything.

The Central Coast NECA chapter represents contractors across both counties, while separate San Luis Obispo and Santa Barbara JATCs deliver the apprenticeship locally. Apprentices earn while they learn, attend class while working and receive 8,000 hours of OJT. That structure is an asset: regional demand can be coordinated while local worksite relationships stay local.

The story

Santa Barbara already has rare shared cleanroom assets. The workforce question is how employers turn them into a technician pipeline.

CC-PRIME links Santa Barbara City College, UCSB cleanroom facilities and local technology companies to train job-ready micro/nanotechnology and semiconductor technicians. The 2026 Quantum Industry Showcase drew 131 participants and 28 companies.

UCSB has also expanded hands-on manufacturing training, including a one-week cleanroom course and a 60-hour Microchip Fabrication I course. The missing layer is recurring employer ownership of job families, paid work-based learning and placement outcomes.
