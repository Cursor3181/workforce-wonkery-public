The Inland Empire's logistics scale, existing job-quality concerns and zero-emission transition create cross-employer problems that isolated recruitment projects cannot solve. The partnership case is strongest where employers, labor, workers and infrastructure partners jointly manage transition occupations and standards.

Worker value

What workers should gain

Worker Value is the intervention. Existing logistics jobs vary in quality, so the partnership should be judged by wage progression, stability, worker voice and movement into high-road zero-emission transportation, electrical, charging, maintenance and infrastructure careers.

The move

Use electrification and technology transition to define a small set of shared occupations and formalize employer-labor-worker co-governance around them.

Local operating story

Electrification is already being treated as a workforce problem.

Plug In IE paired training with fleet-electrification roadmaps developed with CALSTART for union logistics operators and with research on the worker and community impacts of logistics electrification. The missing layer is clearer multi-employer ownership and recurring employer investment in the transition agenda.

Evidence, risks & 90-day action plan

Backbone / host: Inland Empire Labor Institute / Plug In IE

90-day action

0–30 days: Define the transition occupations linking freight electrification, charging, maintenance and existing logistics work. 31–60 days: Secure at least two fleet/employer co-leads and map technology timelines, job changes, wage standards and paid pathway capacity. 61–90 days: Connect union/apprenticeship and high-road training routes to the employer transition map with explicit job-quality measures.

Year-one test

Paid transition pathway; employer-labor co-governance; measurable wage/advancement outcomes; recurring backbone support.

Evidence gaps

Multi-employer agenda ownership; recurring employer investment; paid transition outcomes; worker advancement; technology adoption timing.

Stop / redesign if

Do not build a warehouse recruitment pipeline that mainly increases labor supply without improving wages, stability, advancement or worker voice.
