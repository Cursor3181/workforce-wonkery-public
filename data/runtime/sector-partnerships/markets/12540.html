Worker value

What workers should gain

Bakersfield-Delano solar photovoltaic installers have a $28.14/hour median and Kern mobile/heavy-equipment mechanics a $32.71/hour median in current EDD evidence. Worker Value should be judged project by project through wages, advancement, incumbent access and labor standards, not inferred from the technology label.

The move

Create one verified project-to-workforce portfolio and assign employer action owners to shared technical needs.

Local operating story

The project portfolio is real enough to require discipline.

Kern County reports permitting the first carbon-capture-and-storage project in California, along with large solar and battery-storage portfolios. Its 2026–2030 Strategic Plan explicitly prioritizes carbon management, renewable fuels and workforce pathways to quality jobs. The partnership's job is therefore not to manufacture enthusiasm. It is to distinguish committed projects from speculative demand and align training accordingly.

Evidence, risks & 90-day action plan

Backbone / host: B3K Energy & Carbon Management / Energy Advisory Council

90-day action

0–30 days: Separate committed project schedules and hiring demand from announced or speculative projects across CCS, renewable fuels, solar, storage and industrial energy. 31–60 days: Select technical job families shared across multiple projects and map wages, incumbent skills, training supply and worksite requirements. 61–90 days: Name at least two independent employer/project leads and align training only to the verified demand map; include worker/labor input on progression and job quality.

Year-one test

Employer-owned action plan; verified demand map; technical pathway utilization; living-wage placement/progression; recurring support.

Evidence gaps

Named employer action owners; verified project-to-occupation demand; worker progression outcomes; placement/wage outcomes; recurring backbone support.

Stop / redesign if

Do not build cohorts from announced projects without hiring and timing commitments. Redesign or pause any pathway where project delays remove near-term demand.

Agriculture & food systems

Suggested geography: Kern County / Bakersfield-Delano agriculture and food-processing system

Why it remains visible: Kern has a formal California Jobs First agriculture strategy, farmworker workforce funding, employer-connected internship and career-exposure systems, and explicit plans for ag-tech training and higher-skill advancement. The region has the institutions and funding needed to convene.

What the targeted review found: The agriculture strategy still assigns regional convenors and workforce, education, labor and community partners to identify workforce gaps, develop pilot training, and build internships. The Kern County Farm Bureau participates, but current public evidence does not identify two independent agriculture or food-processing employers jointly owning one recurring workforce bottleneck.

Reopen when: At least two named employers jointly define and own the same hiring, training, advancement, automation, skilled-equipment, food-processing or technology-adoption problem. Until then, use the existing Kern Coalition and ETR infrastructure to convene rather than score a new partnership.
