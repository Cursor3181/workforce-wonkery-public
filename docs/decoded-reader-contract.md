# Workforce Wonkery: Decoded Reader Contract

**Public website implementation standard | October 10, 2026**

**Policy. Data. Practice. Decoded.** This is a reader promise, not decorative copy.

The canonical editorial and human-approval standard lives in the protected Workforce Wonkery source repository at docs/editorial/decoded-brand-standard.md. Public website work implements approved content in that spirit; it does **not** approve new research, invent outcomes, or bypass private publishing controls.

## What a staff reader should get

1. **The insight:** A real, memorable question or operating tension. Avoid clickbait or generic slogans.
2. **Evidence in plain English:** Explain the decisive number or source, who and what it measures, and what it cannot prove. Make limitations visible.
3. **How it works:** Describe actual steps, owners, partners, resource constraints, failures and transferability, not a catalog of programs.
4. **One useful action:** A decision, meeting question, safe pilot or professional tool.

For substantial Practice content, support three reading depths:
- **30-second Decode:** shift, evidence, limit, what to try. A short first-screen summary, not a large colored callout.
- **3-minute story:** who did what differently and why a workforce professional should care.
- **Deeper evidence and implementation:** research limits, methods, operations, source links, California application and relevant working aids.

These are editorial goals, not user-tested completion guarantees. Content should remain readable when sections are skipped.

## Wonkery style

Be concise, curious, human, assumption-testing and evidence-led. Use the approach of a well-written workplace-psychology essay, **not an imitation of Adam Grant or another living writer**. Prefer short paragraphs, precise verbs, meaningful contrast, and practical decisions. Never manufacture dialogue, figures, sources, quotes, causal claims or conferences attended.

## Professional Desk Aids

In two minutes or less, help the reader locate **Use when / Enter / Read the result / Decide / Stop and escalate**. Show real functioning downloads only when they exist, and offer a working example without real participant identifiers. The visual presentation must make high-stakes limitations and source updates easy to find. An Excel formula passing tests is not proof of practitioner usability.

## Site implementation rules

- Preserve the approved visual system and progressive disclosure; do not add unnecessary cards, novelty UI or course-completion mechanics.
- Link to genuine primary sources and qualified evidence statements supplied in approved content; keep dates and caveats.
- Mobile, keyboard, reading flow, focus visibility, accessible table scrolling, download behavior and print layout must work.
- Private-owned editorial research and draft material remain private until explicitly approved for publication.
- Public PR and CI are **not publication**. Approved site changes flow through reviewed public main, allowed private import, governed WordPress Publisher, authenticated readback and live QA.
- AI/code agents should consult this file before making new reader-facing content templates, presentation components or article layouts.

**Editorial stop:** A page has not been fully Decoded if a busy staff member cannot explain its key insight and next action, or a desk aid requires the author to interpret it. Record that feedback in the private review process; do not claim human acceptance from automated checks.
