# Practice portfolio focus and retirement map
Date: 2026-10-09
Status: Public source proposal; **not a live WordPress publication**.

## Editorial focus
The Practice landing page leads with three reader products:
1. Practice Deep Dives: issue-based comparison and synthesis, with identifiable sources, evidence limits, and California translation.
2. Desk Aids & Toolkits: decision-specific working references, checklists and tested templates.
3. Specialized Role Desks: narrow, California-specific tasks where ongoing currency can be maintained, beginning with ETPL.

## Navigation retired in this proposal
- Workforce Foundations Course.
- Workforce in Practice 16-module course.
- AI Readiness Lab.
- General course-completion, progress and assessment messaging on the Practice hub and global footer.

These destinations remain intact for preservation and link continuity until the private publisher has an approved redirect and replacement inventory. Retired from navigation **does not mean deleted, redirected, or unpublished**.
