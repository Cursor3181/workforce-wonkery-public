# Structural Shift editorial standard

Beginning with the October 2026 California Workforce Intelligence Report, include one recurring **Structural Shift** feature.

## Required questions

1. What is changing?
2. Where is California likely to feel it?
3. Which occupations, industries, workers, or systems are affected or exposed?
4. What should WDB staff watch next?
5. What remains uncertain?

## Editorial rules

- Lead with one decision-relevant observation, not a catalog of links.
- Use quantitative evidence to establish scale or direction.
- Pair it with qualitative or implementation context.
- Distinguish exposure from outcome, and correlation from causation.
- Do not turn an exposure measure into a layoff, wage, or job-loss prediction.
- Keep the feature concise enough to remain a recurring part of every issue.

## October 2026 starting topic

**AI exposure is becoming measurable, but exposure is not the same as displacement.**

Use the August 27, 2026 BLS AI exposure categories as the national occupational screen, the existing Workforce Wonkery Technology Watch as the operational interpretation layer, and California-specific demand evidence before drawing training implications.
