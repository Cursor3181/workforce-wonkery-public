# Policy brief Implementation & Impact standard

Use this module for major policy items that unfold across more than one implementation stage.

## Reader question

What has happened since the policy was issued, what happens next, and when should we expect evidence of impact?

## Timeline stages

1. Authority issued
2. Effective or implementation date
3. State guidance or implementation milestone
4. Local action or reporting milestone
5. Earliest reasonable point for outcome evidence

## Status language

Use factual labels such as:

- Issued
- Effective
- Guidance released
- Implementation underway
- Local action due
- Reporting begins
- Outcome evidence not yet available
- Outcome evidence available

Do not use the timeline to score the policy, rate a political actor, or infer a motive.

## Evidence rules

- Each dated milestone must have an official source.
- If the policy is proposed rather than enacted or final, state that directly.
- Separate expected implementation from observed implementation.
- Separate implementation evidence from outcome evidence.
- Do not claim impact before the underlying evidence can support it.

## Recommended placement

Place the module after the brief's bottom-line explanation and before detailed operating guidance when the sequence materially helps a WDB reader understand what to do next.
